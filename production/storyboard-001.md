# Episode 001 Storyboard

## Clap, Clap, Count With Me!

**Format:** 16:9 landscape  
**Target length:** 60–90 seconds  
**Audience:** Preschool children  
**Visual style:** Bright, calm, simple 2D animation with original characters

| Scene | Approx. time | Visual action | On-screen text | Audio |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| 1. Welcome | 0:00–0:08 | Friendly original bunny waves beside the Nextkids logo. | `Nextkids` | AI voice: “Hello, little friends! Are you ready?” |
| 2. One clap | 0:08–0:16 | Bunny claps once, with one star appearing. | `1` | “One little clap—clap, clap, clap!” |
| 3. Two taps | 0:16–0:24 | Bunny taps two colourful blocks. | `2` | “Two little taps—tap, tap, tap!” |
| 4. Three smiles | 0:24–0:32 | Three friendly animal faces smile one at a time. | `3` | “Three big smiles, one, two, three!” |
| 5. Count together | 0:32–0:43 | Numbers 1, 2, and 3 gently bounce in sequence. | `1 2 3` | First chorus |
| 6. Four and five | 0:43–0:53 | Four balloons and then five balloons appear. | `4` then `5` | “Four and five—hooray, you see!” |
| 7. Movement | 0:53–1:08 | Bunny takes steps, makes tiny hops, and waves. | `Step`, `Hop`, `Wave` | Verse 2 |
| 8. Final count | 1:08–1:18 | All five numbers appear around the character. | `1 2 3 4 5` | Final chorus |
| 9. Goodbye | 1:18–1:28 | Characters wave; logo returns. | `Great job!` | Outro |

## Animation rules

- Keep each movement slow enough for a child to copy.
- Use one main action per scene.
- Keep numbers large and visible for at least two seconds.
- Avoid strobe effects, rapid cuts, frightening faces, and cluttered backgrounds.
- Use original characters and artwork only.

## Asset list

- Original bunny character: front, waving, clapping, stepping, hopping
- Three supporting animal faces
- Soft pastel background
- Numbers 1–5
- Four balloons and five balloons
- Nextkids logo treatment
- Original thumbnail showing bunny, numbers 1–5, and the words `COUNT WITH ME!`
