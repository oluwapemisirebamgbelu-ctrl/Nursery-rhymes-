# Nextkids Video Production Workflow

## 1. Plan

1. Choose one learning goal.
2. Write an original lyric or story.
3. Define the target age range.
4. List every asset needed: voice, music, characters, backgrounds, text, and thumbnail.

## 2. Create

1. Record the voice in a quiet room.
2. Create original artwork in Canva or another editor.
3. Edit scenes in CapCut or an equivalent editor.
4. Add large captions and simple movement.
5. Keep the first videos between 60 and 120 seconds while testing audience retention.

## 3. Review

- Is the speech easy to understand?
- Is the pace comfortable for preschool children?
- Are the colours and movement pleasant rather than overwhelming?
- Are the lyrics, melody, recordings, images, and fonts original or licensed?
- Is the educational goal clear?
- Has an adult watched the complete video?

## 4. Export

- Prefer 1080p where practical.
- Watch the exported file from beginning to end.
- Check that audio is not distorted and captions are readable on a phone.
- Use a clear filename such as `nextkids-001-clap-clap-count-with-me.mp4`.

## 5. Upload

Prepare a truthful title, description, thumbnail, and playlist. Set the audience accurately. If the video is directed at children, mark it **Made for Kids**.

## 6. Learn and improve

After publishing, record views, average view duration, audience retention, and returning viewers. Improve one thing at a time rather than changing the entire format after one upload.
