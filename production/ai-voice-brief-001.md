# Licensed AI Voice Brief — Episode 001

## Voice direction

- Warm, friendly, clear English voice suitable for preschool learning.
- Calm energy; cheerful but not shouting.
- Medium-slow pace with clear pronunciation.
- Brief pauses after each number and movement instruction.
- No imitation of a real person, celebrity, or existing cartoon character.

## Suggested delivery

Read the intro and outro conversationally. Read the song lyrics rhythmically, leaving room for children to repeat the numbers and actions.

## Licensing requirements

Before using the generated audio in a public or monetized YouTube video, confirm that the selected voice provider's plan allows:

- Commercial use
- YouTube publishing
- Monetization
- Use in children's content
- Download and retention of the audio
- Use by the Nextkids channel

Save the provider name, plan name, generation date, license terms, and receipt in the project records. Do not use a free plan unless its terms clearly grant the required rights.

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Pronunciation of numbers 1–5 is clear
- [ ] No glitches, repeated words, or unnatural pauses
- [ ] Voice is louder than music
- [ ] No copyrighted voice imitation
- [ ] Commercial license verified
- [ ] Final WAV or high-quality MP3 saved privately before editing
