# Nextkids Content Safety and Rights Checklist

## Before production

- Write original lyrics, or verify that any public-domain source is genuinely public domain in the relevant jurisdiction.
- Create an original melody, or keep proof of a valid music license.
- Use original recordings rather than downloaded versions of familiar songs.
- Confirm permission for every voice, image, animation, font, sound effect, and video clip.

## Before upload

- Check the final video for accidental copyrighted material in the background.
- Do not use famous characters, logos, or another creator's distinctive artwork without permission.
- Keep licenses and receipts in a private project folder.
- Mark the audience accurately. Content aimed at children generally needs the Made for Kids setting.
- Avoid scary imagery, dangerous challenges, unsafe imitation, personal-data requests, and manipulative calls to action.

## Monetization note

Meeting subscriber and watch-time numbers does not guarantee acceptance into the YouTube Partner Program. YouTube also reviews the channel for policy compliance and original, valuable content. Repetitive or mass-produced videos may be rejected even when they receive views.

This file is a practical checklist, not legal advice. Recheck YouTube's current policies before publishing or applying for monetization.
