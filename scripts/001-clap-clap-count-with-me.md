# Clap, Clap, Count With Me!

**Series:** Nextkids Original Songs  
**Episode:** 001  
**Language:** English  
**Audience:** Preschool children  
**Suggested length:** 60–90 seconds  
**Learning goal:** Count from 1 to 5 and follow simple movement instructions.

## Original lyrics

### Intro

Hello, little friends! Are you ready?
Let's clap and count together!

### Verse 1

One little clap—clap, clap, clap!
Two little taps—tap, tap, tap!
Three big smiles, one, two, three!
Come and count along with me!

### Chorus

Clap, clap, count with me,
One, two, three—so happily!
Clap, clap, count with me,
Four and five—hooray, you see!

### Verse 2

One small step, then one more step,
Two tiny hops—don't lose your breath!
Three bright waves for you and me,
Count them all: one, two, three!

### Chorus

Clap, clap, count with me,
One, two, three—so happily!
Clap, clap, count with me,
Four and five—hooray, you see!

### Outro

You counted five! Great job today!
Wave goodbye and shout, “Hooray!”
See you soon, our friends so bright—
Nextkids says, “Goodbye, goodnight!”

## Visual direction

- Use original, friendly animal characters.
- Show large, readable numbers 1–5 on screen.
- Match each action to the lyric: clap, tap, smile, step, hop, and wave.
- Keep movement slow and easy for preschool children to follow.
- Avoid flashing effects and overly busy backgrounds.

## Audio direction

- Record an original vocal performance.
- Use a simple, original melody and a gentle tempo.
- Add soft percussion only if it is original or properly licensed.
- Keep the voice clear and louder than the background music.
